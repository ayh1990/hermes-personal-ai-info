Terms of use

Hermes Personal AI is a private installation for its owner's personal tasks.

Authorized use

The owner connects accounts they are authorized to access and assigns the tasks the assistant may perform. Google account permissions and the owner's task instructions determine the available operations. The installation is not offered as a public multi-user assistant.

Task instructions and review

The owner defines limits and approval requirements. The assistant should follow those requirements, verify results and stop when essential information or permissions are missing. The owner remains responsible for deciding which generated recommendations and proposed changes to use.

Account services

Google and configured AI services remain subject to their own terms, policies and availability. The owner can disconnect an account or stop the local assistant at any time. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

This site

This site supplies public app and OAuth information. It does not provide account registration, a public assistant session or a paid service.
