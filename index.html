Google Workspace integration

A private assistant for personal work.

Hermes Personal AI is an independently configured personal installation of Hermes Agent. It runs on its owner's computer and connects to the owner's Google account through a Desktop OAuth client.

Why Google access is requested

Gmail

Read relevant conversations, organize messages and prepare or send replies according to the owner's assigned task.

Calendar

Read schedules and create or update the events the owner asks to manage.

Drive

Find, read and maintain the files and folders needed for assigned personal workflows.

Sheets

Read trackers, calculate results and update the specific ranges assigned by the owner.

Access is controlled by Google consent and the owner's permissions. The assistant uses local AI by default and can use configured cloud review when a task needs it. This public site provides OAuth information; the assistant is accessed through its private installation.

Data and control

Read the privacy policy for storage, optional cloud processing and revocation. The terms describe the intended personal use. The operator's contact email is shown on Google's OAuth consent screen.
